RookeepaFree bookkeeping that runs in your browser

Small-business bookkeeping, with nothing to subscribe to.

Rookeepa does the everyday accounting a small business actually needs — invoices, bills, contacts, bank reconciliation, GST and financial reports. It is free, open source, and it runs entirely in your browser: your books never leave your computer, because there is no server to send them to.

What it does

  • Invoices, quotes and credit notes — GST handled per line, your logo at the top, and a journal preview before you post anything.
  • Bills, supplier credits and payments — what you owe, to whom, and when it is due.
  • Bank reconciliation — import a statement CSV, categorise the lines, match them against your books, and let your own rules do the repeat ones.
  • Recurring transactions — rent and subscriptions raised as drafts on schedule, for you to check before they reach your books.
  • Statements of account — a printable statement for any customer, showing what they owe and how the balance moved.
  • Real double-entry — a general ledger, a journal, and reports that reconcile to their control accounts.
  • GST and an activity statement summary — the BAS boxes worked out from your own ledger, ready to check with your accountant.
  • Jobs — track a cost or a sale against a job, per line, so one supplier invoice can carry materials for two jobs and the books still keep them apart.
  • Attachments — keep the supplier's PDF or a photo of the receipt against a bank line. The files live in your browser and travel in their own export, so a books backup stays the size of the books.
  • Backups you do not have to remember — point Rookeepa at a folder once and it writes your books and your files into it on a timer. Point a second computer at the same folder — a laptop and a desktop, work and home — and it opens the newest backup from there, with each computer writing its own file so they cannot overwrite each other. (Chrome and Edge on a desktop; see below.)
  • Ten reports, on either basis — profit and loss, balance sheet, trial balance, aged debtors and creditors, cash flow, comparisons against the previous period or the same time last year, and a cash-basis view of the profit and loss.

Why it is different

Free, and open source

MIT licensed. There is no paid tier, no per-seat pricing and no data plan. You can read every line of it.

Local-first

Your books are stored in your own browser and backed up as one JSON file you own. No telemetry, no analytics, no third parties.

Yours to keep running

It is a static site with no build step and no dependencies. Clone it, host it yourself, and it keeps working even if this page disappears.

What it is not

  • It is not connected to the ATO and does not lodge anything. It is a calculation aid — you or your accountant lodge.
  • It is not tax advice. Check the figures before you rely on them.
  • Your books live in one browser. Clearing site data or using a private window loses them, so export the backup file regularly and keep it somewhere safe — that file is your company's records. The optional automatic backup into a folder of your own needs Chrome or Edge on a desktop and only runs while Rookeepa is open: Safari and Firefox have no way for a page to write to a folder, and a browser cannot write to your disk in the background. Everywhere else, the export buttons are the answer.
  • It is a young project. Keep your own records as well, and tell us when something is wrong.
